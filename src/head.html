Pawhold

A clan of foxes, pandas, rabbits and bears settles a green valley. Raise a medieval town, wall it in, and hold it against wolves, boars and badger rams as your age advances from thatch to cannon.

  • Settlers gather wood, stone, berries and gold on their own, and build whatever you place.
  • Pick a building below, then click the ground. Drag to pan, right-drag or the arrows to turn, scroll to zoom.
  • Hover any build card, or any building in the valley, to see its role and how it helps.
  • Click an animal to select it (shift-drag boxes several, or press Army). Right-click to move, attack or send settlers to work. Defenders level up from kills and learn an ability at level 3.
  • Hurt animals heal near the Great Hall and Healing Huts. Idle settlers repair damaged buildings once a raid is over.
  • Walls and towers hold raiders where your archers can reach them. Raids grow every minute or so.
  • Days run from sunrise to sunset to night, and the year turns from spring through summer, autumn and winter, with rain, storms, snow and fog to match. Seasons change your harvest. Use the Time, Season, Weather and Theme buttons to pick your own.
  • Survive 15 raids to complete the chronicle.
